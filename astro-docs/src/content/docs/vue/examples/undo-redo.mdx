`undo()`/`redo()`/`canUndo`/`canRedo` are all exposed on the `GridLayout`
instance, the same `ref`-based pattern as `compactNow()`/`scrollToItem()`.
A snapshot is captured at the *start* of a drag/resize and committed at
its *end* — not per intermediate frame — so one undo reverts a whole
gesture at once, not one step per pixel moved. `undoHistoryLimit`
(default `50`) caps how many snapshots `undo()` can step back through.
