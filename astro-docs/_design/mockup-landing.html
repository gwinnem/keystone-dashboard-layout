Everything the grid does

01
Drag & resize

Every edge and corner, with min/max size constraints per item.

02
Responsive breakpoints

Auto-generate a layout per breakpoint, or hand it exact ones.

03
Pluggable compaction

Five built-in strategies, or bring your own ICompactor.

04
Multi-select & group move

Select several items, drag one — the rest move by the same delta.

05
Cross-grid drag

Drag items between separate grid instances with accept/reject rules.

06
Snap & alignment guides

Magnetic snapping that adjusts position, not just a visual overlay.

07
Persistence & presets

Serialize a layout, or save and switch between named presets.

08
Keyboard accessible

Arrow keys move, shift+arrow resizes — no mouse required.

09
TypeScript-first

Every prop, event, and enum typed and exported from one entry point.

10
Undo / redo

Opt-in history at committed-change granularity, not per frame.

11
Zero dependencies

Native Pointer Events, no bundled drag library to audit.

12
SVG export

A standalone snapshot of the current layout, no screenshot tooling.

13
RTL mirroring

Anchor edges, resize direction, and drag math all correctly reverse.

14
Framework-agnostic core

Positioning and collision logic usable standalone, no framework needed.

15
Battle-tested

98%+ coverage, Playwright e2e across three browsers, mutation testing.

Static & locked items
